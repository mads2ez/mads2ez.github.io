Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026

In short: TrickBag has no account and no server. Your tricks, lists and sessions stay on your iPhone. If you allow it, we get usage statistics that contain nothing you typed. The free version shows a Google AdMob banner. The app does not ask to track you across other apps.

Who we are

TrickBag is made by an independent developer. Contact: maximsivtsevdev@gmail.com.

Data that stays on your iPhone

Your trick lists, your own tricks and their names, sessions, landings and settings are stored only on your device. We never receive them. They are included in your iCloud or computer backups if you use backups, and they are deleted when you delete the app.

Usage statistics (Amplitude)

To understand how the app is used and make it better, TrickBag sends usage events to Amplitude, an analytics service. The data is stored in Amplitude's data center in the United States.

  • What is sent: events such as “app opened”, “onboarding finished”, “session ended” with counts (how many tricks were marked, how long the session lasted), “sticker shared”, and the result of a purchase or restore; the app version and platform; the month you installed the app; your device region and app language from iPhone settings; your riding discipline and rough skill level chosen at first launch; whether ads were shown.
  • Identifier: a random ID created by the app on install. It is not your Apple Account, not the advertising identifier and not linked to your name.
  • What is never sent: anything you type (trick names, list names, search), your location, your IP address (IP collection is turned off), your contacts or photos.
  • Turning it off: TrickBag → Settings → Share usage stats. After that no new events are sent. While it is off, the analytics service is not started at all when the app launches.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a banner from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect data such as your IP address, device information, ad interactions and an approximate location derived from the IP address. TrickBag does not ask for permission to track you (App Tracking Transparency), so the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not available to Google through the app.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choice before personalized ads are shown. You can change it any time: TrickBag → Settings → Ad privacy.
  • How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • After you buy “Remove ads”, the app no longer shows or requests ads.

Purchases

The “Remove ads” purchase is processed by Apple. We do not see your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether the purchase belongs to your Apple Account.

Photos

If you save a session sticker, the app asks for permission to add an image to your photo library. It cannot read your photos.

Children

TrickBag is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access or delete data about you. Because statistics are linked only to a random ID, we can act on a request only if you can identify that ID; the simplest way to stop all collection is to turn off Share usage stats, buy “Remove ads” or delete the app. Write to maximsivtsevdev@gmail.com with any question.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
