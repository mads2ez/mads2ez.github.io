Support

Found a bug, missing a trick, or have an idea? Write to maximsivtsevdev@gmail.com. Tell us your iPhone model, iOS version and the TrickBag version (Settings → About → Version). We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

I bought “Remove ads” but still see ads. Open Settings in TrickBag and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.

I got a new iPhone. Can I keep my purchase? Yes. Install TrickBag, open Settings and tap Restore Purchases.

Will my tricks move to the new iPhone? TrickBag has no account and no server: your lists and sessions live only on your iPhone. They come along when you move to a new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one. If you delete the app, they are gone.

How do I stop sharing usage statistics? Settings in TrickBag → turn off Share usage stats. No new events are sent after that.

How do I change my ad consent? If you are in a region where consent is asked (for example the EU or the UK), open Settings in TrickBag → Ad privacy.

How do I get a refund? Apple handles payments and refunds: reportaproblem.apple.com.
